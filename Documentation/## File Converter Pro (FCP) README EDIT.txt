## File Converter Pro (FCP)

### What is File Converter Pro?
File Converter Pro is a Windows program that lets you convert and handle a wide range of files directly on your computer. Your files stay private, FCP processes your files locally and never uploads them to an online service. It supports images, videos, audio, PDFs, Office documents, 3D models, archives, and more.
An internet connection is only required when you choose to check for updates.

# The story behind FCP

FCP wasn’t built on a whim but was born from frustration with existing online converters that imposed restrictions, locked features behind paywalls, or forced users to upload their files to external servers. 

FCP began as a simple browser-based tool called Simple Image Converter, initially supporting conversions between JPG, WEBP, PNG, HEIC/HEIF, AVIF, ICO, PDF, and SVG, though it wasn’t publicly available. 
As I saw more potential in the project, I looked for ways to broaden its capabilities. The first major upgrade was adding support for video formats like WEBM and MP4, but reliable video conversion in the browser proved challenging. This led me to move the project to my local computer, where I installed the necessary dependencies and spent many evenings experimenting, troubleshooting, and finding solutions to make video conversion work smoothly. After a long time i finally had it working as how i wanted it.
